Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Glug is built on a simple idea: knowing wine is just about knowing what you like. The more you log, the more we learn about you and the better we get at recommending the right bottles. This policy outlines what we collect.

Who we are

Glug is operated by Glug, located at 140 Riverside Blvd, New York, NY. If you have questions about this policy, reach us at privacy@glug-wine.com.

What we collect

When you create a Glug account and use the app, we collect the following:

  • Phone number and name — used to create and identify your account. Your sign-in code is delivered by SMS.
  • Email address and password — only if you choose email sign-in instead of your phone number.
  • Wine ratings and taste preferences — the core of your Glug profile, used to generate personalised recommendations. This includes your logs: ratings, tasting notes, food pairings, and prices you record.
  • Photos of wine lists and bottle labels — the camera is used when you scan. Photos are uploaded to a private storage area that only your account can access (see Data retention below).
  • App usage data — how you interact with the app, such as scan activity, stored in our own database and used to improve the experience. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or payment information.

How we use your data

  • Create and maintain your Glug account
  • Generate personalised wine recommendations based on your taste profile
  • Improve the app's accuracy and performance over time
  • Send you important account-related messages (not marketing, unless you opt in)

AI processing

Glug uses Anthropic's Claude API to read the wine lists and bottle labels you photograph. When you scan, your photo is sent to Anthropic's API to identify the wines on it; your name, phone number and taste profile are not included in these requests. Your recommendations are generated by Glug's own matching system.

We do not use your data to train AI models. Data sent to Anthropic is processed under Anthropic's API terms — see Anthropic's privacy policy.

Third-party services

Glug uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policy:

  • Anthropic — AI processing of the wine list and bottle label photos you scan
  • Supabase — secure database, sign-in, and private photo storage
  • Twilio — delivers the SMS verification codes used to sign in
  • Apple — iOS app distribution (App Store and TestFlight) and device-level permissions
  • Google — Android app distribution (Google Play) and device-level permissions
  • Google Workspace — internal dashboards we use to operate Glug receive aggregate usage statistics and display names, never phone numbers or email addresses

We do not sell your data to any third party and do not share it with advertisers.

Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Photos you scan are stored in private storage and are not automatically deleted while your account is active; deleting a menu scan from your scan history also deletes its photos, and all of your photos are deleted when you delete your account. Your taste profile and ratings are stored as long as your account exists.

Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent for data collection

To delete your account, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account within the app. This immediately and permanently removes your account and its personal data, including your profile, logs, preferences, scan history and scanned photos. You can also email us to request deletion directly.

Age

Glug is an alcohol-related app intended for users aged 21 and over in the United States, or the legal drinking age in your country. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below the legal drinking age. If you believe an underage user has created an account, contact us at privacy@glug-wine.com and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

We are in early development. Our data practices may evolve as the app grows. We will update this policy and notify users of any material changes before they take effect. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.

Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns: privacy@glug-wine.com
